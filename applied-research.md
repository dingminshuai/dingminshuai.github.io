# Applied & Community Research

At the **Bureau of Sociological Research, University of Nebraska-Lincoln**, I lead and manage survey-based policy research and program evaluation for state agencies, nonprofit organizations, universities, and community partners. I have served as project manager or methodological lead on approximately twenty externally funded studies and have produced or co-produced eighteen applied and methodological reports.

Selected work includes:

- **Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services - Statewide Behavioral Health Consumer Survey.** Project Manager for five consecutive annual cycles; study implementation, data processing, quality control, analysis, and methodological reporting.
- **Nebraska Department of Education - Indicator 14 Post-School Outcomes.** Project Manager for federally required post-school outcome data collection involving more than 200 school districts.
- **Illinois Department of Public Health - Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS).** Methodological analysis of frame composition and weighting effects on health estimates.
- **Southeast Nebraska Indigenous Awareness and Engagement Survey.** Community-engagement research involving the Otoe-Missouria Tribe, university researchers, the City of Lincoln, and community partners.
- **American Lung Association / Metro Omaha Tobacco Action Coalition.** Community survey research addressing tobacco use, secondhand smoke, and local public-health policy.
- **Agriculture and conservation research.** Project management and methodological work on agricultural operator, grazing, soil health, conservation, and field-day surveys, including research-methods guidance for a doctoral student project.
- **UNL Sustainability Culture, Literacy, and Transportation Survey.** Campus-wide applied survey research.
- **Nebraska Department of Education - DIVAS.** Multi-year education survey research and reporting.

Across these projects, my work spans study design, sampling, questionnaire development, multimode data collection, quality control, statistical analysis, reporting, budgets and timelines, and communication with agency and community partners.
