# Public Writing & Communication

Public-facing writing has remained part of my work alongside academic research. Before entering public administration, I worked as a journalist and columnist in China. I continue to write for audiences outside the academy and to translate research and policy questions across professional, public, and cultural contexts.

## Policy Commentary

In 2019, writing under the name **Hui Zhou**, I published two invited policy commentaries for *The Echo-Wall*, a project of Heidelberg University's Institute of Chinese Studies:

- **"Efficient" China**
- **When China's Long Game Short Circuits**

## Urban Public Scholarship

From 2021 to 2023, I produced Chinese-language videos and essays through **Cities in a Shell** on the histories, institutions, politics, planning traditions, and civic cultures of major American cities.

I have also written **book reviews and critical essays for Humanities China**, bringing scholarly and intellectual debates to a broader readership.

## Evidence for Public Audiences

At the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, I am a **founding contributor to the Nebraska Snapshot Series**, which translates survey and administrative evidence into concise, accessible accounts of social conditions for statewide audiences.

My academic research on U.S. mayors and public communication, my earlier service as a media and public-opinion specialist for the Long Island Chinese American Association, and my work communicating findings to government and nonprofit clients have further shaped how I write for different audiences.
