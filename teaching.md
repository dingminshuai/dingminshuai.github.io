# Teaching

My teaching is student-centered and applied. I want students to begin with questions they care about, learn how to investigate them, and leave a course better able to continue learning on their own.

## University Teaching

From 2018 to 2022, I served as an independent instructor in the School of Public Administration at the University of Nebraska at Omaha, teaching **eight undergraduate sections** across four academic years.

- **Introduction to Urban Studies** - five online sections
- **Introduction to Public Administration** - face-to-face, hybrid, and online sections
- Average of available quantitative teaching evaluations: **4.53/5** across five reportable sections

For my online courses, I independently designed the full course structure and content, including readings, assignments, assessments, discussion activities, and the sequence of the semester. I have also been involved at the broader program level in online education.

## Community-Engaged Urban Inquiry

In Introduction to Urban Studies, I developed a semester-long project in which students selected a city they knew and investigated an urban issue that genuinely interested them. Students drew on photographs and direct observation, public data, online community forums, archives and planning documents, scholarly literature, and conversations with people familiar with the issue. Projects included the legacy of redlining in Omaha and efforts to improve walkability in Lincoln.

The purpose was not to produce one correct answer. I wanted students to learn how to turn something familiar into a researchable question, judge different kinds of evidence, and connect local observations to larger debates in urban studies and public policy.

## Post-Ph.D. Teaching and Research Mentoring

Although my primary appointment since 2022 has been research-based, teaching and research mentoring have remained part of my work.

- **2025:** Research-methods guidance for an agricultural economics doctoral student conducting a grazing survey through UNL's Bureau of Sociological Research.
- **2024:** Invited research talk on **Research Method and Research Design**, Research Alliance at New York University.
- **2024:** Graduate Research Methods guest speaker, University of Maryland Baltimore County.
- **2024:** Featured presentation on my dissertation research at the International Conference on Local Public Governance and Development, Tunghai University, Taiwan.

My applied research experience also gives me a large set of real cases for teaching research methods, program evaluation, local government, public management, and evidence-based decision-making.
